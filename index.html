How Movie Night picks

Every film in the library carries a “taste vector” — scores for thrills, scares, laughs, heart, darkness, romance, scale, coziness, smarts and weirdness. Your quiz picks teach the app what's distinctive about your taste, then it looks for well-known films that fit — and keeps the list varied. Couples mode only suggests films that clear a minimum fit for both of you, then favors the fairest match.
