How Movie Night picks

Every film in the library carries a “taste vector” — scores for thrills, scares, laughs, heart, darkness, romance, scale, coziness, smarts and weirdness. Your quiz picks build a matching vector, and we recommend the films whose vectors point the same way. Couples mode averages two vectors and gives extra weight to whatever you both love.
